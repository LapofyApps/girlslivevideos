What you need

  • A phone, tablet or laptop with a web browser.
  • This page (or the homepage simulator) open in a tab.
  • A friend with a good sense of humour.

That's it. No app, no account and no phone number. Every call is simulated on your screen.

7 steps to the perfect fake call prank

1. Choose your prank type

A fake video call gets the biggest reaction. A fake incoming phone call is subtler and easier to act out. A fake chat works best for screenshots and group chats.

2. Pick the right profile

Use the filters to find a profile that supports your call type. Choose a name and city that fit your story.

3. Prepare the page

Open the simulator before your friend arrives and scroll to the profile so the call is one tap away.

4. Plan your lines

Decide what you'll say when the call "comes in". A short line like "Wait, I have to take this!" sounds natural.

5. Pick your moment

Timing is everything. A quiet moment, a lull in conversation or right after someone says "nobody ever calls you" works well.

6. Play it out

Tap the button, let the screen ring, then answer. Keep it brief — 20 to 30 seconds is plenty.

7. Reveal the joke

Turn the screen around, point to the "simulated call" label and enjoy the reaction. A quick reveal keeps the prank friendly.

3 ready-made prank scenarios

  • The surprise "celebrity" call: act starstruck as a video call rings, then reveal it was a simulator.
  • The birthday call: stage a mystery call during a birthday dinner, then hand over the real present.
  • The rescue call: agree with a friend in advance that a fake call is your secret "time to go" signal.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Dragging the prank on until it stops being funny.
  • Pranking someone who is stressed, unwell or likely to panic.
  • Faking emergencies, bad news or calls from real, named people.
  • Posting a reaction video online without your friend's permission.
