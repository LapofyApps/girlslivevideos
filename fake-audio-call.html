What is a fake incoming call?

A fake incoming call is a phone call that isn't really happening. Our fake audio call simulator shows an incoming voice call screen with the caller's photo, name and location, an animated ringing status and answer and decline buttons. When you answer, a call timer starts counting just like a normal phone call. No number is dialled, no SIM is used and nobody is on the line.

Because it looks like an ordinary voice call rather than a video call, a fake audio call is often more believable. People are used to seeing a friend take a quick phone call, so nobody looks twice.

When a fake phone call comes in handy

  • Escaping awkward moments: a date that isn't going well, a pushy salesperson or a conversation that won't end.
  • Pranking friends: "answer" a call from a mysterious caller and watch your friends get curious.
  • Leaving politely: step out of a long meeting or a family gathering with a believable reason.
  • Practising: rehearse answering calls confidently if phone calls make you nervous.

How to fake an incoming call

🔇 Make it convincing: the simulator is silent, so play your normal ringtone from your phone's sound settings at the same moment, or simply say "Sorry, I have to take this" as the call screen appears.

Tips for a believable fake phone call

  • Have a short script ready ("Hi! Yes, I'm on my way...") so you don't hesitate.
  • Keep the call short. Real "urgent" calls rarely last more than a minute.
  • Turn the screen away once you've answered, so nobody notices the timer.
  • For a prank, reveal the joke quickly and enjoy the reaction.
