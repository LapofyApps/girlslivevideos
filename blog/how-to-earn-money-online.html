How to Earn Money Online Making Prank & Reaction Videos (2026)

By GirlsLiveVideos Team · March 15, 2026 · Updated October 27, 2026 · 7 min read

Prank and reaction videos are some of the most-watched clips on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook. If you enjoy making people laugh, they can also become a way to earn money online. This guide covers what it really takes: the kit you need, how creators get paid, and the rules that keep your channel (and your friendships) safe.

⚠️ Reality check: there's no guaranteed income here. Most creators earn little or nothing for the first few months. Treat it as a fun side project that can grow, not a quick payday.

Why prank and reaction videos work

Short-form platforms reward videos that people watch to the end and share. A good prank has a built-in hook (the setup), tension (will they fall for it?) and a payoff (the reaction and the reveal) — all in under a minute. Reaction moments are also universal: you don't need to speak a particular language to enjoy a friend's face when a surprise video call rings.

What you need to start

  • A smartphone with a decent camera. You don't need professional gear to begin.
  • A free editing app to cut clips, add captions and music.
  • A simple prop — for example a fake video call simulator that makes your phone show a realistic incoming call.
  • Willing friends who are happy to appear on camera.

5 ways prank creators earn money

1. Platform ad revenue

YouTube, Facebook and some other platforms share advertising revenue with creators who join their partner programmes. Each platform sets minimum follower and view requirements, and they change over time, so check the current rules before you plan around them.

2. Short-form bonuses and creator funds

Some platforms run bonus programmes for Shorts or Reels in selected countries. Availability varies a lot, so treat these as a bonus rather than your main income.

3. Brand deals

Once you have an engaged audience, brands may pay you to feature their product in a video. Smaller "micro" creators with a loyal niche audience often get deals sooner than you'd expect. Always label sponsored content clearly.

4. Affiliate links

Recommend the phone stand, ring light or microphone you actually use with an affiliate link and earn a small commission when viewers buy.

5. Selling your skills

The editing, captioning and thumbnail skills you build are in demand. Many creators earn their first steady money by editing videos for other channels.

Harmless prank video ideas

  • The surprise video call: a "mystery caller" rings during a group hangout. Film the reactions, then reveal it's simulated.
  • The fake excuse call: show how a fake incoming phone call rescues you from an awkward moment.
  • The chat that replies too fast: screen-record a fake chat with comically instant replies.
  • Prank-the-pranker: a friend tries to prank you, and you turn the tables with your own fake call.

Platform rules you must follow

  • No harmful pranks. Platforms remove pranks that make people think they're in real danger or cause genuine distress — and may demonetise or ban the channel.
  • Get consent before posting. Ask everyone who appears in the video, and blur anyone who says no.
  • Be honest with viewers. Show the reveal and mention that the call was simulated.
  • Never impersonate real people. Don't present a fake call or chat as coming from a real, named person.
  • Keep minors out of risky content. Platforms have strict rules for videos featuring children.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really earn money from prank videos?

Yes, but most creators earn little at first. Income usually comes from platform ad revenue, brand deals and affiliate links once you have a consistent audience, which takes months of regular posting.

Are prank videos allowed on YouTube?

Light-hearted pranks are allowed, but YouTube and other platforms remove pranks that make people believe they are in serious danger or cause real emotional distress. Always read each platform's current community guidelines.

Do I need to say a fake call is simulated?

It is good practice. Revealing the joke on camera and mentioning that you used a simulator keeps viewers' trust and avoids misleading anyone.
